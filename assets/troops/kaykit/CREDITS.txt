The files in this directory are vendored byte-for-byte for runtime use (not
re-exported or modified). Survival dresses the garrison with them at runtime
(src/game/troop3d.js): the Knight's door guards keep the round shield and get
the one-handed crossbow on the right hand slot; the relief column keeps the
one-handed sword and the badge shield. The other weapon meshes in Knight.glb
are hidden, never deleted from the file. The garrison wears the keep's royal
blue: at load, a canvas COPY of the Knight's embedded atlas has its saturated
reds (the cape and tabard) shifted to blue (troop3d.js, blueLivery); the GLB's
bytes and its embedded texture stay as shipped.
