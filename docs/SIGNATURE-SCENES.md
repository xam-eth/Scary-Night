# LAST NIGHT — Visual Gameplay Pillars (the hero scenes)

*Owner-authored, 2026-09-30. The visual product identity. These are the shots that,
in 1–3 seconds, make a stranger understand the game — and therefore the **primary
visual target for the build**, not "make a nice 3D mansion." Companion to
`docs/BRIEF.md` (systems/visual rules) and `docs/PURPOSE.md` (retention spine).*

## Product identity (one line)
**"I am a monster hunter defending my last fortress through increasingly impossible
nights."** We do not copy one game — we take each genre's visual shorthand and fuse it:
Vampire Survivors → **horde spectacle**; Clash of Clans → **visible fortress
progression**; racing → **aspirational, visible equipment**; Darkwood → **preparation +
night tension**.

Protagonist: **Valen**, an armed monster-hunter (sword + crossbow + lantern). *(The name
Valen is kept; the role is the hunter of `docs/BRIEF.md §2`, not a vampire.)*

## The 4 primary visuals (the agent's main visual target)
If these four images make a stranger say *"I get it — fortify the mansion, then survive
the monsters until dawn,"* we have found the visual identity. **Build the game so these
four read instantly:**
1. **THE SIEGE** — dozens of monsters storming the mansion, Valen at the center.
2. **THE FORTRESS** — the mansion transforming from an empty house into a hunter's keep.
3. **THE ALPHA** — the boss silhouette; every monster freezes and looks one way.
4. **THE LAST MINUTE** — Valen near death, the house near ruin, dawn seconds away.

## The 7 hero scenes (storyboard order = the trailer)

### 1. THE SIEGE — *sells: survival* — 🔨 build the density/staging
Top-down 3D. Center: Valen with sword/crossbow. **20–40 creatures** pour in from every
direction — crawling from halls, hammering doors, climbing windows, chasing Valen,
tearing barricades. HUD: `DAWN IN 02:17` · `SERVANT DOOR ██████░░░` · `BLOOD 41%`. Valen
stands between **two doors both about to break**. *Our version of the VS "surrounded"
shot:* **ONE HUNTER. ONE FORTRESS. AN ENTIRE NIGHT TRYING TO BREAK IN.** — Key visual #1.

### 2. THE FORTRESS — *sells: building / progression* — 🔨 build (the retention linchpin)
**BEFORE → AFTER**, not a combat shot. Night 1: empty, dark, plain wood doors, little
furniture, one small candle, Valen alone. After progression: **reinforced doors,
barricades, silver symbols, candles, traps, a weapon station, furniture moved into
defenses.** Valen at the center of a place that has become *hers*. *"I didn't just
upgrade numbers — I changed the place I defend."* **This is the visible progression
engine (`docs/PURPOSE.md` P6) — the Clash-base equivalent, stronger than an abstract
meter.**

### 3. THE PREPARATION — *sells: strategy* — 🔨 build (the signature opening)
Before the night: Valen walks the mansion; the player sees `DOOR A ████████ · DOOR B
██████ · WINDOW BROKEN · TRAP READY · PLANKS x4` and **chooses which door to reinforce.**
Valen places a barricade (**THUNK**), sets a silver trap (**CLANK**), loads the crossbow
(**CLICK**), lights the lantern (**WHOOSH**). Then: `11:00 PM` — every light goes out.
The signature opening scene.

### 4. THE HUNTER — *sells: character fantasy* — ✨ polish
Close-ish 3/4 gameplay camera. Valen: sword in hand, crossbow on back, coat moving,
lantern, blood on the coat, breathing. She hears something — **KNOCK** — turns; the door
shifts — **KNOCK**. Sells *"I am the monster hunter,"* not *"a guy in a mansion."*

### 5. THE ALPHA — *sells: horror / escalation* — 🔨 build the anticipation
Not a boss spawn. **Anticipation, staged:** (1) every enemy stops → (2) they all look one
way → (3) the lights die → (4) **FOOTSTEP** → (5) a huge **silhouette** at the end of the
hall → (6) HUD `THE ALPHA` + a health bar → (7) Valen raises the crossbow; the Alpha
charges; **CUT.** The trailer's *"what the hell is that?"* beat.

### 6. THE LAST MINUTE — *sells: tension* — ✅ mostly exists (crescendo)
HUD: `DAWN IN 00:43` · `BLOOD 18%` · `HOUSE 27%` · `PLANKS 0`. One door broken, one nearly,
~15 enemies, Valen bleeding, lantern flickering, crossbow mid-reload. `00:42` dash →
slash → door breaks → enemy in. `00:31` run. `00:19` werewolf. `00:08` blood 4%. `00:03`
silence. `00:02` sunlight. May hit harder than the boss.

### 7. DAWN — *sells: victory / payoff* — ✅ exists (dawnbreak), needs the quiet
Not a victory screen — a **reward visual**. After the chaos: no music, no enemies,
sunlight through the windows, Valen alone in a **ruined** room (smashed furniture, blood,
broken door, dead candles) — but alive. HUD: `DAWN · SURVIVED 05:00`. Emotional payoff,
maximum contrast with the scene before.

## The two transformations — PROGRESSION → VISIBLE (retention)
- **Weapon transformation.** `HUNTER'S BLADE → SILVER BLADE → BLESSED BLADE → DAWNBREAKER`
  — **the model changes on the body**, not just a UI tier. Valen returns to the mansion
  holding a visibly different weapon.
- **Mansion transformation.** `ABANDONED HOUSE → SAFE HOUSE → FORTIFIED HOUSE → HUNTER'S
  KEEP → THE LAST FORTRESS`. Not necessarily five full levels for the MVP, but the
  **visual language is designed now** so the fortress visibly climbs these states.

## The content engine
Because the game speaks in hero scenes, every future update yields a ready-made piece of
content — a fantasy shot, not a "NEW UPDATE!" card: **new weapon** → weapon reveal ·
**new enemy** → enemy intro · **new mansion** → environment reveal · **new boss** → boss
reveal · **new defense** → before/after fortress · **new chapter** → new room unlocked ·
**event** → the mansion under siege.

## The 30-second trailer (beat sheet)
`0–3s` dark mansion, **KNOCK**. `3–6s` Valen opens her eyes, picks up the sword. `6–10s`
barricades a door, loads the crossbow — text **SURVIVE UNTIL DAWN**. `10–15s` doors
hammered, a crawler in, Valen strikes. `15–20s` **WIDE SHOT** — dozens of monsters fill
the mansion, Valen at center — `DAWN IN 02:14`. `20–24s` everything stops; silence; the
Alpha silhouette. `24–27s` Valen fires; the Alpha charges. `27–30s` sunrise; ruined
mansion; Valen still standing — **HOW LONG CAN YOU SURVIVE? / LAST NIGHT**.
