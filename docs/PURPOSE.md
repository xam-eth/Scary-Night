# LAST NIGHT — The Purpose Spine (retention / "why return")

*The meta-loop. Owner-locked direction (2026-09-30): **THE HUNT**. This doc is the
source of truth for **why a player comes back** — the layer above the moment-to-moment
survival. `docs/BRIEF.md` governs the core loop + visual; this governs retention.*

## 0. The problem this fixes
LAST NIGHT had a strong **core loop** (survive tonight — tension, juice, climax
peaks) and almost no **meta loop** (a reason to return tomorrow). The only pull was
"survive longer" — a score, the weakest retention hook there is. Grounded in
Self-Determination Theory (competence / autonomy / relatedness) and the roguelite
retention canon (Hades, Dead Cells, Vampire Survivors), the fix is a **purpose spine**:
a destination, death-that-becomes-progress, a relationship, and story unlocked by play.

## 1. Core fantasy — THE HUNT
You are **the Hunter**. You did not wander in — you came to **kill the thing that owns
this house** (the **Master**). On night one it is far too strong to face. Every night
you survive, you **learn its patterns, take its weaknesses, and arm yourself** — until
you can corner it and end it. The nights are not endless survival; they are **a hunt
closing in**. There is an end: kill the Master, and get out. And there is a personal
stake — the Master took someone of yours; that is why *you*, specifically, came.

## 2. The five pillars (each proven in the games we researched)

### P1 — Destination (there is an END)
The goal is **kill the Master**, not survive forever. A visible **HUNT TRACK** advances
as you progress; at a threshold the Master becomes huntable → the **final confrontation**
(the Boss-Duel climax peak becomes this). Survive-until-dawn is the *means*; the hunt is
the *why*. A game with an ending gives every night meaning.

### P2 — Death = progress (the #1 retention engine — the Hades/Dead Cells law)
**Every night — win OR lose — yields persistent hunt progress. A night is never wasted:**
- **Intel** — you learn a piece of the Master (its nature, a weakness, where it nests,
  when it walks). Seeing a new enemy or room in a run you *lost* still banks intel.
- **Materials / trophies** — from what you killed or survived, toward weapons that can
  actually hurt the Master (silver, blessed bolts, wards).
- **The track moves** — a "closing-in" meter that only goes forward.
This turns **"TRY AGAIN" into "I'm one step closer to it."**

### P3 — A hub + ONE relationship (relatedness — the Hades secret, most absent today)
Between nights, the **HUNTER'S REFUGE** (a dawn safehouse) holds **one character who
remembers and reacts**: an **informant** — a survivor trapped in the house, or a fellow
hunter on the other end of a radio/journal — who knows the Master, feeds you intel,
responds to your runs, and whose **own arc and fate deepen as you progress**. You return
to them at dawn. **You come to care whether they make it.** This is the single biggest
retention lever, and the thing the game most lacks.

### P4 — Story through play (the "why" is earned, not dumped)
What the Master is, why it takes people, why you came, and how to end it — all unfold as
the **HUNT TRACK advances**, milestone by milestone, never front-loaded (Hades reveals
its full story only after ten clears). The personal-stake twist lands near the finale.

### P5 — Three clear goal layers (Vampire Survivors clarity)
- **Tonight (short):** a hunt objective — *find where it nests · survive the Master's
  stalk · recover the silver · mark the beast*. A concrete reason THIS night matters
  beyond survival.
- **This stretch (mid):** the next weapon tier / intel milestone / the informant's next
  reveal — the "next unlock" pull.
- **The Hunt (long):** corner and kill the Master → the ending.

## 3. How the frozen systems fold in (repurposed under the spine, not revived as-is)
- **Economy** → the **death-into-progress engine**: currency/materials feed weapon tiers
  and intel, all serving the hunt (not an abstract compulsion economy). Bank-vs-risk
  stays, but the "purse" is now **hunt materials** toward killing the Master.
- **Narrative** → **story-through-play**: dawn-cards / codex now reveal the Master and the
  informant's arc as the track advances (relational + earned), not detached mood beats.
  `docs/STORY.md`'s house-as-prison mood survives as texture; the *spine* is the hunt.
- **Enemies** → each is a facet of the Master's domain: the werewolf/stalker are its
  hounds / lesser forms — killing and studying them **is** intel, not random waves.
- **The 4 climax peaks** → the **Boss Duel** becomes the real hunt confrontations; the
  crescendo/frenzy serve the nightly hunt tension.

## 4. The felt arc (what changes for the player)
Night 1 *"just survive."* → Night 3 *"I'm learning this thing."* → Night 7 *"I'm hunting
it now — I have the silver."* → Finale *"tonight I end it"* — and the informant's fate
rides on it. That is **competence** (I grow and master), **autonomy** (how I hunt and
arm myself), and **relatedness** (the informant + the personal stake) — all three SDT
needs, unified by one purpose.

## 5. Minimum viable spine (what to build first, in order)
1. **HUNT TRACK + death-into-progress** — a persistent meter + a per-night "what you
   gained" screen (intel/materials/track), shown on **every** death and dawn. *(This
   alone converts the loop from score-chase to progress — build it first.)*
2. **The informant + the refuge hub** — one character, a dawn hub screen, lines that react
   to the track. Relatedness.
3. **Tonight's hunt objective** — a short goal surfaced at night start + resolved at dawn.
4. **The finale** — the Master becomes huntable at the track threshold; the Boss-Duel peak
   is the kill. An ending.
5. **Weapon tiers / intel milestones** — the mid-term unlock ladder feeding P2/P5.

## 6. Guardrails
- The spine must **not** slow the fast restart or bloat into an RPG (brief §3). One
  meter, one hub, one relationship, three goal layers — lean.
- Death-into-progress is a **hard rule**: no run ever yields zero hunt progress.
- Story stays *earned through play*, gothic and restrained — never a front-loaded dump.
- Mobile-first; sessions still resolve in minutes; the hub is quick.
- Reuse the existing systems (economy/narrative/director/peaks) repurposed here — do not
  fork them. Real, tested, no placeholder.
