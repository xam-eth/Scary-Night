tools/survival-balance.mjs grew two measurement flags for this pass:
  --rot=<N2>,<N3>    force the rotation indices a run plays (into NIGHT_ROTATIONS)
  --plan3=<gates>    force the Night 3 gate order itself, to measure a round the table does not carry

the row says otherwise. The archetype that decides these limits is arms-builder (a barracks and
the guards' crossbows, no ballistas, no towers): the weakest defence that still wins BUILD 16's
nights 5/5.

Two limits fall out of that table:
  * a 110-HP postern as wave 2 lost 0/3 twice (northGate>posternNW..., westGate>posternSE...).
    The same postern as wave 1 holds 3/3: the first wave arrives while the garrison is still whole,
    and 30 bodies through a broken postern at 10% of the night are answerable; at 30%, with the
    defence committed to wave 1's gate on the far side of the bailey, they are not.
  * a step of more than a quarter of the wall circuit between wave 1 and wave 2 lost too
    (posternNW>eastGate and posternSE>westGate, both 135 deg: 0/3 and 1/3), while every 45/90 deg
    step measured held 3/3.
And one shape was dropped outright: four 30-body waves at four big gates (no postern). arms-builder
only survived it when Night 2 happened to prep it — 3/3 behind Night 2 variant 0, but 1/5 behind
variant 2 and 3/5 behind variant 3 (--rot=2,3 / 3,3 with the first table, 5 seeds). It is not in
the shipped table.
Night 2 keeps its wider shapes: 24/27/27 bodies with a postern as wave 2 measured 3/3 there (the
rot 0,0 and 1,0 rows below), so the limit is Night 3's (30 per wave, two Breakers).
