LAST NIGHT

BRAND KIT · SURVIVE UNTIL DAWN

The identity of a quiet horror: candlelight against ink, gold against bone. Everything here is generated from tools/brandkit.mjs — edit that file, re-run it, and every asset below is rebuilt. The plates and the mark are the source of truth; this page is the rulebook.

01 · THE MARK

The mark is a door in a ring of night: the crescent moon (the night that owns her), the arched servant door (the choice — bar it or open it), the candle window (hunger, light, life), and one drop of blood below the threshold. It reads at 32 px and it reads on a billboard; never redraw it, never recolor it.

Clearspace & minimum size

  • Clearspace on all sides equals the moon’s diameter (≈ 12 mark-units).
  • Minimum size: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Below that, use the ring + door only.
  • On photography, place the mark only over shadowed areas; add a 40–60% ink shade behind it if needed.

Never

  • Never rotate, skew, outline, drop-shadow or gradient-fill the mark.
  • Never swap the blood drop for red brighter than Blood #7d1220 — the horror is quiet.
  • Never place type inside the ring, and never paint a second title over the plates.

02 · LOCKUPS

The vertical lockup is the default. The horizontal lockup is for wide placements (banners, headers, press). The wordmark alone is for captions and watermarks. Bone over ink is the house style; the ink variant exists for light paper only.

Vector: logo-vertical.svg · logo-horizontal.svg · wordmark.svg. Transparent PNG: vertical · horizontal (+ -ink variants).

03 · COLOUR

Ink carries ~70% of every composition. Bone and ash are the voice; gold and candle are the light; blood and moon appear only as accents (~3%). If a layout needs more red than the drop in the mark, it is not LAST NIGHT.

04 · TYPE

Display — Gelasio / Georgia, tracked caps. The wordmark sets letter-spacing at 0.16 em; the tagline at 0.40 em. Headlines always set in caps with 0.10–0.22 em. Never bold the display face — hierarchy comes from size, tracking and gold.

Labels — Lato / Segoe UI. Small caps labels, buttons and chips (0.30–0.45 em tracking), never larger than 26 px in a 1080 px composition.

Data — Consolas / Menlo. Clocks, seeds, telemetry: the house keeps records. Mono appears in product UI, not in marketing.

05 · VOICE

The brand speaks like the house: spare, ominous, honest. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no hype, no emojis. The tagline is a promise, not a shout.

  • “Survive until dawn.” — the only call to action that matters.
  • “The house remembers.” / “Something knocks.” — mystery lines for teases.
  • “Play free in your browser.” — the plain, honest CTA for store & social.
  • Never “EPIC”, “EXTREME”, “#1”, “jump-scare” language. The horror is psychological.
  • Never explain the monster in marketing. Imply, never expose.

06 · ART DIRECTION

Two registered styles. Key art is the painted plate: a gothic house at night, one lit window, fog, moonlight — photoreal, desaturated, ink-shadowed. Night motif is the procedural silhouette (moon, spires, pines, fog) used for wide banners where no plate exists. Both keep the 70% ink rule.

Do not paint a second title on top of the plates — they already carry the lockup.
