HOLD UNTIL DAWN

A manor fortified against the night. One lantern keeps watch while indistinct shapes gather beyond the walls; the last strip of dusk sits low on the horizon.

Wordmarks

Carved, high-contrast serif lettering with one restrained crescent detail in the O. The outlined SVGs do not rely on a font installed on the viewer’s device.

Key art · portrait

The clean plates are UI-ready. Branded counterparts place the lockup in the quiet top zone; all four files are 1080 × 1920.

Key art · wide plates

One original master scene, recomposed for the website hero, social card, and store feature graphic.

Design principles

Hold, not hunt

The manor is the hero; the siege remains a scale of darkness, not a portrait or a readable opponent.

Atmosphere over gore

Cold blue dusk, one warm lantern, boarded openings, and a narrow horizon line carry the dread.

Phone first

The title lockup stays in the calm upper area, while the art and emblem remain legible at small sizes.
